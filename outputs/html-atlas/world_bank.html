World Bank Data Used by the Atlas

World Development Indicators supply the GDP baseline, population, undernourishment, and poverty values used by the 2026 atlas.

FieldIndicatorRole
GDP per capitaNY.GDP.PCAP.CDG₀ baseline and regression outcome
PopulationSP.POP.TOTLRegression weight and national aggregation
UndernourishmentSN.ITK.DEFC.ZSLever 9 input
Extreme povertySI.POV.DDAYLever 9 input

Download and rebuild

Each indicator can be downloaded as CSV, XML, or Excel from the World Bank Data portal. The build pipeline uses the public World Bank API through wbgapi and stores processed snapshots under data/processed/. Run python3 -m src.fetch_wb for a small connectivity check, then the canonical atlas build command.

Vintage and missingness: “2026 baseline” means the freshest published value available when the atlas was built, not that every observation was measured in 2026. Missing undernourishment or poverty is conservatively scored as lever 9 absent. Current-US$ GDP is appropriate for the atlas's current-dollar totals but includes exchange-rate and price-level movement.
