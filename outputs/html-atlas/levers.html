The Nine Levers: Fields, Thresholds, and Capital Exclusions

Clicking a lever from the atlas opens the exact source fields and binary rule used to populate it. The dollar amount is that lever's globally attributed share after the national 25% cap is allocated.

What is the NCM Isolated Entity?
NCM Isolated Entity is not a V-Dem variable. In this atlas it names the focal unit of analysis: one UN member state observed for one reference year, temporarily treated as a bounded entity so its nine conditions and GDP can be compared consistently. The country is not assumed to be causally isolated; trade, geography, resources, history, conflict, and regional systems remain outside this reduced cross-section.
Lever 1

Legal Protections

$2.78T annually

Present when ordinary law is applied consistently to state agents as well as everyone else. Absent when power can act without predictable legal constraint. Multiplies GDP by lowering the risk of arbitrary loss; people and businesses invest and trade more when they know the rules will be enforced fairly. Annual cost is low.

RTLP question

Does the entity have provisions explicitly protecting the right to life?

Proxy: Transparent laws with predictable enforcement

Transparency note: Operational de-facto proxy; it is not a direct constitutional right-to-life field.
Lever 2

Independent Judiciary

$2.83T annually

Present when there is a judiciary capable of upholding right-to-life laws even against the state. These protections represent opportunities for property rights and contracts to support reliable capital flows to productive uses. Annual cost is low relative to the scale unlocked.

RTLP question

Executable crosswalk

Proxy: High-court and lower-court independence

Transparency note: The two available court estimates are averaged when either is present.

Lever 9

Socioeconomic Conditions

$1.72T annually

Present when a society makes the deliberate choice to maintain a minimum floor against extreme deprivation. This reflects political will rather than just economic outcomes. When present, it increases the share of the population able to participate productively, expanding overall economic scale. Annual cost is a political decision about minimum security standards.

RTLP question

Are food and minimum material conditions adequate to sustain life?

Proxy: Prevalence of undernourishment and international-poverty headcount

Transparency note: Missing values are conservatively treated as failure. This is an outcome-based basic-needs proxy, not a V-Dem field.
