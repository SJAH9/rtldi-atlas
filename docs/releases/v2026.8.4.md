## Summary

This release makes the atlas's executable lever crosswalk, GDP regression, eta, uncertainty, source datasets, and known limitations directly inspectable in both the HTML atlas and print ebook.

## Highlights

- Nine dedicated lever records expose global annual capital exclusions, source variables, thresholds, proxy choices, and caveats.
- Defines the NCM Isolated Entity as the focal country-year analytical unit, not a V-Dem field or a claim that countries are causally isolated.
- Discloses proxy reuse in the released component data: levers 3 and 6 use `v2clkill`; levers 4 and 7 use `v2xcl_acjst`.
- Reports the canonical population-weighted model: `n=187`, `eta=0.305`, robust `p=0.00568`, weighted `R2=0.252`, with the operational coefficient rounded to `0.30`.
- Separates the strong cross-sectional GDP-level association from the non-significant 2023-2024 current-US$ growth check (`R2=0.004`, `p=0.207`).
- Adds official V-Dem and World Bank WDI guidance, distinguishing the published Full+Others v15 score source from the current v16 update workflow and documenting the missing source checksum as a replication boundary.
- Updates the Eurozone report and embeds its 27 pages after nation profiles and before evidence/data pages.
- Regenerated the complete 278-page ebook; page 8 retains the `$15.87T` global total.
